# MFL-01 — Healthy vs Defective Rope

## Simulation

Measurement point:

- X = 60 mm
- Y = 11 mm

## Results

| Condition | Magnetic Field |
|---|---:|
| Healthy | 0.256471 T |
| Defective | 0.224430 T |

Absolute difference:

0.032041 T

Relative change:

12.49% decrease

## Conclusion

The simplified 2D FEMM model shows that the simulated
rope defect produces a measurable magnetic-field
disturbance at the selected measurement point.

## Engineering Note

This is a proof-of-concept simulation only. The model
does not yet represent the complete 3D sensor geometry,
actual Hall/MR sensor response, lift-off variation,
environmental noise, or real wire-rope construction.
